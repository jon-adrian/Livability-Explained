Initial prototype writeup

What have we done so far?

We created a working structure for an interactive explanation of city livability. Our page includes an introduction, a three-step scrollytelling section, and a future exploration area. The scrollytelling uses Scrollama to detect which explanation is active. A D3 placeholder changes in response to scrolling, and the final section includes a separate interactive city map with metric toggles and hover details.

What will be the most challenging to design, and why?

One challenge will be combining the three livability metrics into a clear comparison without hiding important differences. We also need to decide how to normalize data that comes from different sources and uses different units. Keeping the map responsive as users scroll and adjust their preferences may require careful coordination of state. Finally, we need to explain the limitations of the ranking so that visitors do not interpret it as an objective judgment about cities.
